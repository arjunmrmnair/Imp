# USER CHEAT SHEET: Portfolio Monitoring Prompts

Use these prompts to get the most accurate, data-driven analysis for your Polycab, Eicher, Apar, and South Indian Bank portfolio.

### **1. The "Quarterly Health Check" (Do this every 3 months)**
**Prompt:**
> "Apar, Polycab, and Eicher have released their quarterly results. Check `screener.in` and perform Step 1: Quarterly Health Check. Did they pass the 15% Sales and 20% Profit CAGR rules? Is the investment thesis still intact?"

### **2. The "Concall Decoder" (Do this for qualitative depth)**
**Prompt:**
> "Fetch the latest concall summaries for South Indian Bank and Apar Industries. Perform Step 2: Concall Decoder. What are the management's guidance on capex, demand, and raw material volatility? Are there any hidden red flags?"

### **3. The "Tranche Trigger" (Do this during market crashes)**
**Prompt:**
> "The market is falling significantly today. Check the current prices of my 4 stocks (APARINDS, POLYCAB, EICHERMOT, SOUTHBANK). Based on my initial buy levels (April 2026), perform Step 3: Tranche Trigger. Is it time to deploy Tranche 2 or Tranche 3 capital? Should I buy the dip?"

---

### **4. The "Annual Rebalance" (Do this once a year)**
**Prompt:**
> "Perform Step 4: Annual Rebalance. Check my current portfolio weights. Apar and Polycab have grown significantly. Based on current P/E valuations and growth, should I shave some profits and rebalance into Eicher or a new multibagger candidate?"

---

### **5. The "Red Flag Alert" (Do this for news/rumors)**
**Prompt:**
> "I heard some negative news/rumors about [Insert Stock Name]. Check the TIER 6 Red Flag Elimination Checklist. Fetch data from `screener.in` and tell me if this is a structural failure or just noise. Should I exit or hold?"
