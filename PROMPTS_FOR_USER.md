# USER CHEAT SHEET: Portfolio Monitoring Prompts

Use these prompts to get the most accurate, data-driven analysis for your "Super 5" portfolio (Apar, Polycab, Eicher, Nippon AMC, and BEL) or when hunting for multibaggers.

---

### **1. The "Quarterly Health Check & SIP" (Do this every 3 months)**
**Prompt:**
> "My Super 5 stocks have released their quarterly results. Check `screener.in` and perform Step 1: Quarterly Health Check. Did they pass the 15% Sales and 20% Profit CAGR rules? Summarize their Concalls and News. Based on the results, which stock should I deploy my ₹30,000 Quarterly SIP into today?"

---

### **2. The "Concall & News Decoder" (Do this for qualitative depth)**
**Prompt:**
> "Fetch the latest concall summaries and news for [Stock Name]. Perform Step 2: Concall Decoder. What is the management's guidance on capex, demand, and raw material volatility? Are there any hidden red flags?"

---

### **3. The "Tranche Trigger" (Do this during market crashes)**
**Prompt:**
> "The market is falling significantly today. Check the current prices of my Super 5 (APARINDS, POLYCAB, EICHERMOT, NAM-INDIA, BEL). Based on my initial buy levels, perform Step 3: Tranche Trigger. Is it time to deploy Tranche 2 capital? Should I buy the dip?"

---

### **4. The "Annual Rebalance" (Do this once a year)**
**Prompt:**
> "Perform Step 4: Annual Rebalance. Check my current portfolio weights. Apar and Polycab have grown significantly. Based on current P/E valuations and growth, should I shave some profits and rebalance into Eicher or a new multibagger candidate?"

---

### **5. The "Red Flag Alert" (Do this for news/rumors)**
**Prompt:**
> "I heard some negative news/rumors about [Stock Name]. Check the TIER 6 Red Flag Elimination Checklist. Fetch data from `screener.in` and tell me if this is a structural failure or just noise. Should I exit or hold?"

---

### **6. The "Multibagger Hunter" (Do this with fresh capital)**
**Prompt:**
> "@multibagger-hunter I have ₹50,000 in fresh capital for a high-risk bet. Run the `bag` screener. Apply the adjusted filters (Promoter >= 45%, positive FCF) and find me 1 or 2 small-cap multibaggers with a clear capacity expansion or catalyst in their latest Concall."
