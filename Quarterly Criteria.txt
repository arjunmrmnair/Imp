QUARTERLY CRITERIA - MANDATORY CHECKS FOR EVERY STOCK RECOMMENDATION
Last Updated: After ELGIEQUIP Error Analysis (3-Year Returns Critical Filter Added)

=== TIER 1: HARD STOPS (Any failure = ELIMINATION) ===

✓ 3-Year Stock Price CAGR ≥ 15% [CRITICAL - Root cause of ELGIEQUIP error]
✓ Market Cap ≥ ₹1,000 Cr
✓ PAT ≥ ₹500 Cr
✓ ROCE > 20%
✓ ROE > 15% (5Y average)
✓ Debt-to-Equity < 0.25
✓ FCF (5Y Trend) > 0
✓ Promoter Pledge = 0%
✓ P/E Ratio < 80
✓ Current Ratio > 1.5
✓ Interest Coverage Ratio > 3x

=== TIER 2: BUSINESS QUALITY CHECK ===

YoY Revenue Growth 
  → Target: > 8%, Consistent across quarters
  → Red flag: Declining or volatile

EPS Growth
  → Target: > 15% YoY
  → Should accelerate: 1Y > 3Y > 5Y pattern
  → Red flag: Declining EPS despite revenue growth

Operating Margin (EBITDA Margin)
  → Target: > 15% (Premium businesses > 20%)
  → Direction: Should be stable or improving
  → Red flag: Declining margins = competitive pressure

Net Profit Margin
  → Direction: Stable or improving
  → Red flag: Declining margin while growing revenue = cost structure broken

Return on Equity
  → Target: > 15% (5Y average)
  → Direction: Should be improving YoY
  → Red flag: Declining ROE

Return on Capital Employed
  → Target: > 20%
  → Direction: Should be improving
  → Red flag: Declining ROCE = capital efficiency worsening

=== TIER 3: GROWTH VALIDATION ===

5-Year Revenue CAGR
  → Target: ≥ 10%
  → Red flag: < 8%

5-Year Profit CAGR
  → Target: ≥ 15%
  → Key check: Profit CAGR vs Revenue CAGR ratio
    - If Profit < Revenue: Cost structure deteriorating
    - If Profit >> Revenue: Margin expansion (✓ good)

3-Year Profit CAGR  
  → Target: ≥ 20%
  → Why: Validates that profit acceleration is real, not temporary

YoY Quarterly Sales Growth
  → Target: > 8%
  → Consistency: Should not fluctuate wildly (lumpy = risky)

YoY Quarterly Profit Growth
  → Target: > 1%
  → Trend: Should be consistent quarter-on-quarter

=== TIER 4: FINANCIAL HEALTH ===

Debt Level (Debt-to-Equity)
  → Target: < 0.25
  → Direction: Declining is good; increasing is bad
  → Red flag: > 0.40

Free Cash Flow
  → Target: Positive and growing
  → Direction: Should track with profit growth
  → Red flag: Negative or declining FCF

Asset Turnover
  → Direction: Stable or improving
  → Red flag: Declining = inefficient asset utilization

Quick Ratio
  → Target: > 1
  → Problem: < 1 = liquidity stress

=== TIER 5: GOVERNANCE & MANAGEMENT QUALITY ===

Dividend Yield
  → Good sign: Consistent dividends
  → Better sign: Growing dividends (Management confidence)
  → Calculate: Payout Ratio should be < 50% (leaves room for growth)

Promoter Pledge
  → Target: 0%
  → Red flag: > 10%

Promoter Holding Trend
  → Good: Increasing (Insider confidence)
  → Bad: Decreasing (Insider selling)
  → Neutral: Stable

Insider Trading
  → Good: Promoter buying in last 2 quarters
  → Bad: Promoter selling
  → Context: Understand reason (personal vs loss of confidence)

=== TIER 6: RED FLAGS - IMMEDIATE ELIMINATION ===

❌ 3Y Stock Price CAGR < 15% [CRITICAL]
❌ Revenue declining YoY
❌ Profit declining faster than revenue decline
❌ ROCE declining
❌ Operating Cash Flow < Net Income
❌ Debt increasing while revenue flat
❌ Interest Coverage < 2x
❌ Working Capital deteriorating
❌ Frequent auditor changes
❌ Restated financials in past 3 years
❌ Promoter pledge > 10%
❌ Market share declining for 2+ years

=== VALUATION CHECKS ===

Price-to-Earnings Ratio
  → < 20: Undervalued
  → 20-40: Fair
  → > 40: Overvalued  
  → > 80: Eliminate

Price-to-Book Ratio
  → Target: < 3x
  → Conservative: < 2x

EV/EBITDA
  → Target: < 12x
  → Attractive: < 8x

PEG Ratio (Implicit)
  → P/E should not exceed growth rate
  → Example: 40% growth rate = P/E up to 40x acceptable
    But capped at <80x per hard stop

Margin of Safety
  → Buy if trading 20%+ below intrinsic value

6. PRICE ACTION vs. FUNDAMENTAL STRENGTH
   ✓ If fundamentals strong, stock should NOT crash >30% in 6 months
   ✓ If stock down 40%, either: (a) Market wrong, or (b) Insiders know something

7. CAPEX & CWIP SIZING
   ✓ Check: Capex plans (from earnings call)
   ✓ Check: CWIP size on balance sheet
   ✓ Ratio: CWIP should be < 20% of total assets
   ❌ RED FLAG: Large capex, small CWIP = Future debt burden
   ❌ RED FLAG: CWIP > 25% of assets = Funds locked, productivity delayed

8. PROFIT GROWTH vs. SALES GROWTH DIVERGENCE
   ✓ Normal: Profit CAGR ≈ Sales CAGR (stable margins)
   ✓ Good: Profit CAGR significantly > Sales CAGR (margin expansion)
   ❌ RED FLAG: High sales growth but profit declining = Cost inflation
   ❌ RED FLAG: Profit CAGR >> Sales CAGR but OPM declining = Accounting trick
   
   ALPEX SOLAR Case:

=== KEY MULTIBAGGER INDICATORS ===

✓ Profit CAGR >> Revenue CAGR (Margin expansion - KEY SIGNAL)
✓ ROCE/ROE improving over time (More efficient)
✓ Stock price lagging earnings (Valuation catch-up potential)
✓ Industry tailwinds (Sector growing, company gaining share)
✓ Promoter buying or increasing holdings (Insider confidence)
✓ Dividend growing while capex growing (Strong financial position)
✓ FCF positive and growing (Sustainable growth)

⚠️ WARNING PATTERN ⚠️
Strong profit growth BUT weak stock returns = VALUATION COMPRESSION
Example: ELGIEQUIP - Profit CAGR (5Y: 52%, 3Y: 27%) vs Stock CAGR (3Y: 3%)
→ Shareholders getting NONE of the profit growth
→ Someone else appropriating the value (promoter, pricing power loss to industry, etc.)
→ ELIMINATE if 3Y stock return < 15%
